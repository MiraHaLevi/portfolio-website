Colorplan Digital Campaign

A collaborative project developed with GF Smith through Falmouth University, responding to a live brief to create a one-minute animation and digital campaign for Colorplan paper.

The campaign was designed to inspire the next generation of creatives to explore Colorplan by communicating the qualities that make the paper distinctive, including its extensive colour range, texture, and different weights. Across the campaign, I explored multiple ways of translating these physical qualities into engaging digital content.
