Define The Feeling

A conceptual campaign for Merriam-Webster created in recognition of Mental Health Awareness Month. Define the Feeling uses language as a starting point for encouraging conversations around mental health, particularly among adolescents.

The campaign aims to give young people the vocabulary to recognize and describe what they are experiencing, while making it easier to identify signs of mental health struggles in themselves and their peers. By combining Merriam-Webster's educational identity with an important social issue, the campaign creates an approachable way to encourage awareness, understanding, and conversations around mental health.
