About

I'm a multidisciplinary designer who really likes branding, marketing, and figuring out why people connect with certain things. I like jumping between different kinds of projects, whether that means building a visual identity, figuring out a campaign, designing for digital, or picking up a camera.

I'm still figuring out exactly where design will take me, which is part of the fun. I mostly just want to make things that people actually want to look at, interact with, and remember.
