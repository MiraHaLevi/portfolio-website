The Postcard Journal

An exploration of how journaling can become more approachable, creative, and forgiving by moving it away from the traditional blank-page format. Instead of committing thoughts to a single notebook, the project translates journaling into a collection of postcards, allowing each entry to exist independently without the pressure of filling an entire book or getting a page "wrong."

I created a series of postcards, a reusable postcard template with custom stamps, and a hand-bound instructional book explaining how to create the system yourself.
