The Second Act

A conceptual digital support platform designed for people aged 45–55 navigating a period of significant personal and professional change. Rather than focusing on a single challenge, The Second Act brings together resources for navigating career transitions, health changes, caregiving, and other shifts that can emerge during this stage of life.

The platform is designed as a consolidated space where users can find information, guidance, and support across multiple areas of change, making an often complicated period feel more manageable and connected.
